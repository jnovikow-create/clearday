Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.Clearday

Short version: What you type stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves your plan on the device:

  • The habit you are quitting, your quit date, daily cost, and daily use
  • The reason you wrote
  • Craving logs: time, intensity, trigger, and that you rode it out

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes this save unless it is in a device backup.

2. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Clearday does not receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers those transactions.

3. What this app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics and no tracking across other companies’ apps
  • No sale of your information

4. Children

Clearday is not directed to children under 17. It is a quit tool for adults.

5. Health

Clearday is a wellness tool and does not provide medical advice.

6. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
