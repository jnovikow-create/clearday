How to use it

  • On first open, choose vaping, smoking, or pouches. Set the quit date, what a day used to cost, and why you are quitting.
  • Today shows the live counter, money saved, and units avoided.
  • Craving SOS is a one-minute breathing guide. Pick what set it off, then follow the circle. Use it as often as you need. The banner ad hides while you breathe.
  • Progress lists milestones from 24 hours to one year, and a chart of the cravings you rode out.
  • If you slip, open Settings and reset the counter. Your craving history stays. A slip is not failure.

Ads

Clearday is free. A banner above the tab bar supports it. The banner hides during the one-minute Craving SOS breath.

Common questions

Where is my data?
On this iPhone. There is no account. Deleting the app removes the quit plan and craving log unless they are in a device backup.

Is this medical advice?
No. Clearday is a wellness counter. Talk with a clinician about quitting nicotine.

Something broken?
Email jnovikow@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing.
